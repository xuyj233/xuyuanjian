\section{A Brief Introduction}
I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, Guangzhou campus, supervised by Prof. Guang Zhang from HKUST-GZ and Dr. Zhong Li from MSRA. My past research has focused on \textbf{sample selection and sample quality assessment across the full LLM training lifecycle}. Recently, I have been exploring two directions:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.5em]
  \item \textbf{Multimodal Data Selection:} Studying how data selection strategies should adapt to multimodal corpora with extremely low signal-to-noise ratios.
  \item \textbf{Specialized Text Data Selection:} Understanding what domain-specific data, such as agentic trajectories and long-context documents, contributes to LLM training, and developing principled criteria for selecting examples that provide useful capabilities and learning signals.
\end{itemize}

My work has been published at leading venues including \textbf{NeurIPS 2026}, \textbf{ICML 2026 ($\times$2)}, \textbf{ACL 2026 ($\times$2)}, \textbf{ACL 2025}, \textbf{ACL 2023}, \textbf{ICASSP 2026}, and \textbf{ACM ICAIF 2025}.
I also serve as a reviewer for leading conferences such as \textbf{NeurIPS}, \textbf{ICLR}, and \textbf{ICML (\trophy Gold Reviewer Award)}. I was selected for top talent internship programs at \textbf{Tencent Hunyuan}, \textbf{MSRA}, and \textbf{Tsinghua AIR}.
